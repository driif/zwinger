⌘K shows all actions. Press ⌘K again or Escape to close them and return the focus. A terminal is a Mac shell (on the Mac, with no sandbox), a VM shell or an agent session (in the
cage). The shortcuts in a terminal work only when a terminal has the focus. All ⌃ keys, and the ⌥ and ⇧
key combinations that are not in these tables, go to the terminal and its agent.

Press Tab to focus a notice and read its full text. Press Tab again to reach its actions, if present.
